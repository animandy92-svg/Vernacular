# Vernacular course review packet

Generated from the application content. These are draft learning materials, not native-speaker approvals.

For each language, ask a qualified speaker to check spelling, dialect, translation, context, cultural fit and the written pronunciation guides. Edit incorrect entries at their source. Recheck lesson questions after edits. Never approve an entire pack because one entry has been reviewed.

Record normal and slow versions only after the text is approved. Keep written permission to distribute the recordings. Individual word clips are not supplied in this build. The separate Twi listening library includes the three user-supplied LearnAkan recordings with on-screen translations.

## Twi

20 lessons · 52 distinct words and phrases

Reviewer: __________  Dialect: __________  Review date: __________

### Lesson sequence

- **Your first hello:** Welcome someone and say thank you. Scenario check: Someone arrives at your home. Choose the expression meaning “welcome”.
- **Greetings through the day:** Recognise morning, afternoon and evening greetings. Scenario check: It is the afternoon. Which greeting have you practised?
- **A little courtesy:** Recognise please, sorry, yes and no. Scenario check: You want to add “please”. Which expression means that?
- **A first introduction:** Practise asking how someone is and introducing Ama. Scenario check: Someone asks how you are. Choose “I am well”.
- **Welcome checkpoint:** Bring your first greetings and expressions together. Scenario check: You are practising the introduction “My name is Ama”. Find it.
- **Your close family:** Recognise words for mother, father and child. Scenario check: In a family picture, which word labels “mother”?
- **Brothers and sisters:** Recognise brother, sister and family. Scenario check: You are labelling a picture of a sister. Which expression fits?
- **Visiting grandparents:** Practise grandmother, grandfather and home. Scenario check: Which expression in this lesson means “grandfather”?
- **Friendship and belonging:** Recognise friend, love and name. Scenario check: You want the word meaning “friend”. Which one is it?
- **Family checkpoint:** Remember familiar people without looking back. Scenario check: Choose the word meaning “family”.
- **Food and water:** Recognise water, food, rice and bread. Scenario check: You are identifying rice on a food list. Which word means “rice”?
- **A small shopping list:** Recognise fish, orange and banana. Scenario check: Which word labels the banana in your basket?
- **Count to five:** Practise the numbers one to five. Scenario check: There are three items in the basket. Choose “three”.
- **Market words:** Recognise market, money and price. Scenario check: Which word in this lesson means “money”?
- **Market checkpoint:** Remember food, numbers and money words together. Scenario check: Your list contains bread. Choose its name.
- **Around the home:** Recognise home, room and chair. Scenario check: You are naming a chair. Which word fits?
- **Learning together:** Practise school, book and friend. Scenario check: Which word means “book”?
- **Today and tomorrow:** Recognise morning, today, tomorrow and yesterday. Scenario check: You are referring to tomorrow. Which word means that?
- **Finding your way:** Practise here, there, road, go and come. Scenario check: Which word in this lesson means “here”?
- **Your foundation checkpoint:** Bring greetings, people and everyday words together. Scenario check: Return to your first conversation. Choose “I am well”.

## Adding approved recordings

Place files under `public/audio/<language>/`. Add each approved item to `src/data/native-audio.json`, keyed by the entry ID. The transcript must exactly match the displayed word or expression. The build includes these files in the offline cache and fails if a referenced file is missing.

```json
{
  "entry-id": {
    "normal": "/audio/twi/entry-id.mp3",
    "slow": "/audio/twi/entry-id-slow.mp3",
    "transcript": "Exact approved expression",
    "dialect": "Dialect confirmed by reviewer",
    "speaker": "Credited speaker",
    "reviewedBy": "Qualified reviewer",
    "reviewedOn": "YYYY-MM-DD"
  }
}
```

The example is a schema illustration; do not use placeholder review details in the application. The optional slow file can be omitted; the player then uses slower playback of the normal recording.

After corrections and recordings: `npm test` and `npm run build`. Review the actual course on a phone with a speaker before public educational release.
