# Ga video audio collection

189 individual recordings extracted from the five supplied lesson videos. Each link opens the corresponding audio file.

In the app, select Ga, open the practice games, and choose Listening Challenge. The game plays these recordings and asks for the English meaning. Normal and slow playback are available.

These are attributed lesson excerpts. Ga transcription and clip boundaries still need native-speaker review. Source music may remain. Unclear conversational responses, introductory previews, recaps, commentary and the routine pop quiz are omitted.
